# 👑 Opus Financial - Premium Money & Expense Tracker

Opus Financial is an ultra-clean, minimalist private wealth and smart expense management web application.

## ✨ Key Features
- 📊 **Executive Dashboard**: Net balance, income, expenses, and net savings trend chart.
- 🔐 **Mobile OTP Authentication**: Log in via mobile number (`+91 98765 43210`) with 4-digit OTP verification.
- 🎯 **Credit Score & CIBIL Simulator**: Real-time CIBIL score gauge (785 Excellent) and "What-If" decision simulator.
- 🖼️ **Photo Category Budgets**: Category budget caps with high-res photography cover cards.
- ⏰ **Automated Bill Reminders**: Scheduled bill countdown tracking with 1-click payment logging.
- 👥 **Family & Roommate Bill Splitter**: Shared expense tracking and balance settlement.
- 📈 **Investments & Crypto Portfolio**: Mutual funds, SIPs, stocks, digital gold, and crypto assets.
- 🏛️ **Total Net Worth Calculator**: Real-time asset and liability accounting.
- 💳 **Credit Cards & Rewards**: Limit usage meters, due date tracking, and reward points.
